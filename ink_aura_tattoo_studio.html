Knowledge Base

AFTERCARE & STUDIO FAQ

Everything you need to know about preparing for your session, pricing policies, and preserving your ink.

Our resident artists charge a standard day-rate of $1,200 to $1,800 depending on complexity, or $200/hr for smaller bespoke sessions. A non-refundable retainer deposit of $200 is required upon booking to secure your date and initiate custom design drawings. This deposit is credited directly towards the final session fee.
Get a solid night's sleep, eat a substantial meal 1 hour prior to your appointment, and stay well hydrated. Avoid alcohol and blood thinners for 24 hours prior. Wear comfortable, dark clothing that allows easy access to the tattoo placement area.
1. Leave protective second skin bandage on for 3 to 5 days.
2. Once removed, gently wash the tattoo twice daily with lukewarm water and antibacterial fragrance-free soap.
3. Pat dry with a clean paper towel (never cloth towels).
4. Apply a microscopic layer of recommended aftercare balm (e.g., Hustle Butter) 2-3 times daily. Do not oversaturate.
5. Avoid direct sunlight, swimming pools, baths, and picking at peeling skin for at least 3 weeks.
Yes! We offer one complimentary touch-up within 6 months of your completed session if any minor settling occurs during the healing process (excludes hands, feet, and elbows where touch-ups may incur standard hourly fees).
